## Results

All output files are H.264/YUV420 MP4, 1920×1080, 30 fps, without audio.
One X11 stream captures both panes. Recorded `/clock` receipts map wall-time
frames onto 30 frames per simulated second; playback is 1× sim time.
Bring-up and the gap between parked cells are removed. Window chrome is
cropped, with a 1.4× gz centre crop. Native logs and JSON sidecars remain
beside the MP4s in the artifact directory.

## Verification and observations

- Three final launches, four E2E cases: passed native log checks, zero lockstep
  timeouts. Source logs are `parked-release`, `drive-release`, `match-release`.
- Registered sim unit/lint results: 183 reported tests, zero errors/failures.
  Foxglove layouts were regenerated from both RViz presets.
- Capture refuses a busy ROS domain or an existing capture directory. Render
  refuses a failed test,
  a rejected native log or missing viewer/camera setup. Busy-domain refusal
  was exercised against the live isolated stack.
- `ffprobe` confirms frame counts 2124/759/1013 and 3896 for the reel; all are
  1920×1080 H.264 at 30/1 fps. Rendering decoded the full selected sim windows.
- Sampled frames were checked against native scored-time counters. The final
  RViz view includes robot, map, lidar, tracked armor, aim ray and hit/miss
  traces; gz provides the physical arena view. An early drive take without
  its map was replaced after fixing display-map delivery.
- [RViz chassis orientation and retrospective shot semantics](../README.md#record-an-e2e-demo)
  still apply. Gazebo actors are smaller than the RViz robot/markers because
  its view retains field context.
- Native checkers accept the final runs but retain shutdown notes for
  two `parameter_bridge` aborts and a forced RViz shutdown in parked, plus
  `head_slider_relay` exit 255 in drive. Match shutdown has no checker notes.
  These occur after scoring; no installed stack nodes remain afterward.
