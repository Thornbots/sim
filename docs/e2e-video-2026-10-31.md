## Verification and observations

- The pinned sim's `launch/e2e.launch.py` spawns blue at `(4.625, 0)`.
  Firmware `OdometrySubsystemConstants.hpp` advertises `(3.53, -0.426)`;
  `mcb_firmware.hpp` subtracts that start from world position when supplying
  hosted pod readings. This yields the observed approximately
  `(1.095, 0.426)` raw boot position, magnitude 1.175 m.
- Localization's `scripts/seeded_ekf` rejects raw seed positions whose
  magnitude exceeds 0.3 m when requiring referee team. Its start constants
  also use `(3.53, -0.426)`. This pin incompatibility blocks EKF seeding
  before ingress or combat can test the refactor. No start, timeout, scoring
  gate or firmware override was applied.
- ffmpeg extracted frames at 15 and 25 wall seconds from each of the six
  raw MP4s. All 12 frames were visually inspected: RViz left and gz right
  are both visible and nonblack. RViz shows robot/lidar/aim, while gz shows
  the arena and actors. The localized map is absent during the failed
  bring-up. Samples remain under `frame-check/`. Caption verification and
  30 fps passing-demo checks are unavailable because rendering was skipped.
- The native checker additionally noted RViz exit -11 and
  `head_slider_relay` exit -9 during parked retry shutdown, and
  `parameter_bridge` exit -6 during match attempt 1 shutdown. These do not
  replace the common mid-run EKF failure.
- `install-sim.sh`, the hosted firmware build, the seven portable-package
  colcon build and the Docker skill smoke check passed. Host Python 3.12
  repository quality checks and actionlint results are preserved beside
  the recordings. No baseline was changed.
- Registered sim tests failed `McbFirmware.SimpleDriveRouteAndStageGate`
  at `test/cpp/test_mcb_firmware.cpp:423`: expected translation magnitude
  below 0.05, observed `0.85327374935150235`. The firmware executable's
  other 89 cases passed. Colcon reports 233 results, zero errors and two
  failures because this one failed assertion appears in both the CTest
  wrapper and its GTest report; 22 of 23 CTest targets passed. Logs and XML
  reports are preserved. No baseline comparison was run, so this is not
  attributed specifically to the refactor.
- No third attempt, firmware/control-code edit, workspace gitlink bump,
  nightly/main push, PR or merge was made. Passing renders and the highlights
  concat were skipped because every native log checker rejected its run.
- `agent_container.sh down video-20261031` removed this task's container and
  volumes. The helper found no running containers and stopped Colima. No
